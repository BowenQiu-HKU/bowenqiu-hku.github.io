Bowen Qiu 裘博文

PhD Student · The University of Hong Kong
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering

I am a PhD student at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Ed X. Wu. My research interests include ultra-low-field MRI, quantitative brain imaging, and brain development and aging.

I received my B.E. in Biomedical Engineering from Zhejiang University in 2025, where I was supervised by Mingyang Li and Dan Wu. Previously, I worked as a research assistant at Boston Children’s Hospital, Harvard Medical School, on fetal brain MRI analysis.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal neuroimaging. Neural correlates of crystallized and fluid intelligence in adolescents.
  • Fetal brain imaging. Regional analysis of automated cortical plate segmentation.
